Hi, thanks for the update. A Zoom URL is only generated after the host confirms a specific date/time — just creating a meeting or request isn't enough. This is by design, not a bug, but we've added on-screen hints to clarify it. Two ways to test:

1) Regular meeting: Meetings tab > Create a meeting > add a candidate date > open the meeting > Host menu > tap the candidate date to confirm it (no need to wait for others) > choose "Issue a Zoom link and confirm".

2) 1-on-1: Meetings tab > Create > Request a 1-on-1 > tap "Already arranged a time elsewhere? Request with a specific date/time" > pick date/time > select Zoom > submit. URL appears instantly, before the other person responds.

(First connect Zoom once via My Page > Scheduling Settings > External Integrations.)

Let us know if you hit any issue.
